I am a feminist activist and, since July 2025, a PhD Law Student in Law at **Universidad de los Andes** (Colombia). I previously completed a BA in Public Policy at the **Centro de Investigación y Docencia Económicas (CIDE, Mexico)**.

My research interests revolve around violence, body and the State through the lens of queer feminism. Some of my recent texts are about the impact of anti-discrimination reform on prison law and the denial of torture on judicial rulings. Until today, my doctoral project asks how decarceration strategies for women create or transform prison law.

I have been a contractor for civil society organizations, designing research projects and supervising funding and advocacy strategies. Some of them are GIRE, Dejusticia and CELS, for name a few. I am always interested in support collectives and grassroots organizations. Lately, I have been focusing on designing MEL tools and facilitating annual planning sessions.

My publications include peer-reviewed articles, book chapters, civil society reports, and public writing in outlets such as *Animal Político* and *Volcánicas*. I have also participated as a panelist, commentator, and moderator in international forums, seminars, and conferences on human rights, gender, and drug policy.
