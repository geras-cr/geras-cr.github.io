I am a PhD candidate in Law at **Universidad de los Andes** (Colombia), advised by Dr. Fernando León Tamayo Arboleda. I previously completed a BA in Public Policy at the **Centro de Investigación y Docencia Económicas (CIDE, Mexico)**, graduating with honors and the top average of my cohort.

I currently work as a doctoral graduate teaching assistant at Universidad de los Andes, coordinate the Co-Impact initiative between the **Center for Reproductive Rights** and **GIRE**, consult on fundraising for the **Grupo de Información en Reproducción Elegida (GIRE)**, and serve as an external researcher on drug policy reform at the **University of Essex** (UK).

My academic and advocacy work focuses on gender, sexuality, trans rights, drug policy, and incarceration in Latin America. I have consulted for organizations including the Centro de Estudios Legales y Sociales (CELS), Mexico's Supreme Court (SCJN), Dejusticia, and Equis Justicia para las Mujeres, among others.

My publications include peer-reviewed articles, book chapters, civil society reports, and public writing in outlets such as *Animal Político* and *Nexos*. I have also participated as a panelist, commentator, and moderator in international forums, seminars, and conferences on human rights, gender, and drug policy.
